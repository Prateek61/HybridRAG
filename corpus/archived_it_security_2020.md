Under the former policy, multi-factor authentication (MFA) was **optional** for most systems. Accounts previously locked after **3 failed login attempts**. These authentication rules were later strengthened.

## Passwords (Former Policy)

The previous password standard required a minimum of **8 characters**, changed every **60 days**. Under the old policy, the company password manager was **LastPass**. This former standard no longer applies.

Under the previous policy, security incidents were to be reported within **4 hours** to the IT help desk. The old data classification scheme used only **two levels**: Public and Private.

## Eligibility (Former Policy)

Under the former policy, benefits eligibility began on the first of the month after **60 days** of employment. Open enrollment was previously held from **October 1 to October 31** each year.

## Retirement — 401(k) (Former Policy)

Previously, TechCorp matched **50% of contributions up to 3% of salary**. Under the old plan, the employer match vested after **3 years** of service. These retirement terms were replaced in a later plan year.

## Equity — RSUs (Former Policy)

Under the previous equity program, restricted stock units (RSUs) vested over a **3-year period** with a **6-month cliff**. This former vesting schedule no longer applies.

## Health and Savings (Former Policy)

The former health plan provided a company HSA contribution of **$500 per year**. Sick leave under the old policy was **5 paid sick days per calendar year**. Parental leave was previously **8 weeks for the primary caregiver** and **4 weeks for the secondary caregiver**.

# TechCorp Benefits Guide

This Benefits Guide describes the health, retirement, leave, and wellness benefits available to eligible TechCorp employees. It expands on the summary provided in the Employee Handbook. Benefits described here are available to full-time employees.

## Eligibility and Enrollment

Eligible employees become eligible for benefits on the first of the month following 30 days of employment. New employees must complete benefits enrollment within their first 30 days. After the initial enrollment window, changes may only be made during the annual open enrollment period, which runs from November 1 through November 30 each year, or following a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, or adoption.

## Health Insurance Plans

Eligible employees receive access to medical, dental, and vision insurance plans. TechCorp offers three medical plan tiers:

- **Basic Plan** — Lower monthly premium with a higher deductible.
- **Standard Plan (PPO)** — Balanced premium and deductible, the most commonly selected plan.
- **Premium Plan (PPO)** — Higher monthly premium with the lowest deductible and broadest network.

Dental and vision coverage are included with all three tiers at no additional premium for the employee. TechCorp covers 80% of the employee medical premium and 50% of the premium for covered dependents.

## Health Savings and Flexible Spending Accounts

Employees enrolled in the Basic Plan (a high-deductible health plan) are eligible for a Health Savings Account (HSA). TechCorp contributes $750 per year to each eligible employee's HSA. Employees on any plan may also elect a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) to set aside pre-tax dollars for medical or dependent-care expenses.

## Retirement and 401(k)

TechCorp offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan to all eligible employees. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of the employee's annual salary. Employee contributions are always fully vested. The company match vests after 2 years of continuous service. Employees may begin contributing to the 401(k) on their first day of employment.

## Equity and Stock

Eligible employees receive a grant of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) as part of their compensation. RSU grants vest over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff: 25% of the grant vests after the first year of continuous service, and the remaining 75% vests in equal quarterly installments over the following three years. Additional refresh grants may be awarded based on performance.

## Paid Leave

In addition to the 20 days of paid time off described in the Employee Handbook, eligible employees receive the following leave benefits:

- **Sick leave** — 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO.
- **Parental leave** — 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks of paid leave for the secondary caregiver, available after 6 months of employment.
- **Bereavement leave** — Up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
- **Jury duty leave** — Paid time off for required jury service.
- **Public holidays** — TechCorp observes 11 paid public holidays each year.

## Life and Disability Insurance

TechCorp provides company-paid basic life insurance equal to 2 times the employee's annual salary, along with short-term and long-term disability coverage at no cost to the employee. Employees may purchase additional voluntary life insurance during open enrollment.

## Wellness and Employee Assistance

TechCorp provides wellness support programs, mental health resources, and an Employee Assistance Program (EAP). The EAP includes up to 8 free confidential counseling sessions per year for each employee and their household members. Participation in wellness initiatives is voluntary and confidential.

# TechCorp Equipment Policy

This Equipment Policy describes the company-provided hardware available to employees, the process for requesting additional equipment, and the responsibilities employees have for company-owned devices. Company equipment is intended strictly for authorized business purposes.

## Standard Equipment

All engineers are provided with a MacBook Pro as their standard work device. In addition, every new employee receives a one-time $500 home office stipend to set up a productive remote workspace. The home office stipend may be used for items such as a desk, chair, or lighting and is reimbursed after the employee submits valid receipts.

## Equipment Refresh Cycle

Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if a device fails or no longer meets performance requirements. Employees do not need manager approval for a standard refresh at the end of the 3-year cycle; the IT team initiates the refresh automatically.

## Additional Peripherals and Accessories

Employees may request additional peripherals and accessories — such as an external monitor, keyboard, mouse, or docking station — up to a total of $2,000 with manager approval. Requests are submitted through the IT portal and reimbursed within 10 business days of approval. Purchases above the $2,000 accessory cap require director-level approval.

## Care and Responsibility

Employees must take reasonable precautions to prevent theft, damage, or unauthorized access to company devices. Company equipment must be kept secure and used in accordance with the IT and Information Security Policy. Software installations must comply with company IT guidelines, and unauthorized software is prohibited.

## Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Equipment

Lost, stolen, or damaged equipment must be reported to the IT team within 24 hours of discovery. The first accidental replacement of a device is covered by the company at no cost to the employee. Replacements required due to repeated negligence may result in a partial charge to the employee, at the discretion of the IT team and the employee's manager. Stolen devices must also be reported to local authorities, and a copy of the report provided to IT.

## Loaner Equipment

While a device is being repaired or replaced, employees may request temporary loaner equipment from the IT Service Desk so they can continue working without interruption. Loaner equipment must be returned within 5 business days of receiving the replacement device.

## Returning Equipment

Upon termination of employment, employees are required to return all company-owned equipment, documents, and confidential materials within 5 business days of their final day. The IT team will provide a prepaid shipping label for remote employees. Access to company systems may be revoked immediately following separation, and employees remain bound by confidentiality obligations even after employment ends.

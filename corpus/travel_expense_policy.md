# TechCorp Travel and Expense Policy

This policy describes how employees should book business travel and submit expenses for reimbursement. All business travel must receive prior approval from the employee's manager. Employees are expected to use cost-effective travel options whenever practical.

## Travel Approval and Booking

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before any booking is made. Once approved, employees must book flights, hotels, and transportation through Concur, the company-approved booking platform, whenever available. Employees must comply with all travel safety guidelines.

## Airfare

For domestic flights and any flight under 6 hours, employees should book economy class. For international flights longer than 6 hours, premium economy class is permitted. Business and first class require executive approval and are reserved for exceptional circumstances.

## Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to a cap of $250 per night for domestic travel. Travel to high-cost cities may exceed this cap with prior manager approval. Employees should select standard rooms at company-approved or comparable hotels.

## Meals and Per Diem

Employees receive a meal per diem to cover food costs while traveling:

- **Domestic travel** — $75 per day.
- **International travel** — $100 per day.

The per diem covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Itemized receipts are not required for per-diem meals, but alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Mileage and Ground Transportation

Employees who use a personal vehicle for approved business travel are reimbursed at a rate of $0.67 per mile. Approved ground transportation such as rideshare, taxis, and public transit is reimbursable with receipts. Rental cars must be booked through Concur and should be economy or mid-size class.

## Receipts and Submission

Expense reports should include valid receipts, be submitted within 10 business days of the expense, and clearly describe the business purpose. Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense over $25. Reimbursements are processed and paid within 10 business days of an approved expense report. Unapproved or excessive expenses may be denied reimbursement.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are not reimbursable and remain the responsibility of the employee:

- Alcoholic beverages.
- Personal entertainment, including in-room movies.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Personal travel extensions added to a business trip.
- Expenses without a valid receipt where one is required.

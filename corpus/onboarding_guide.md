# TechCorp Onboarding Guide

This Onboarding Guide helps new employees get set up and productive during their first days and weeks at TechCorp. New hire orientation is held every Monday for employees starting that week.

## Before Your First Day

Before a new employee's start date, the IT team automatically creates a setup ticket to provision accounts and ship equipment. New employees receive a MacBook Pro and login instructions for company systems. Remote employees receive their equipment by mail ahead of their first day.

## Day One Checklist

On the first day, new employees should complete the following:

- Pick up or unbox the MacBook Pro and confirm it powers on.
- Sign in to Okta single sign-on (SSO) and set up multi-factor authentication.
- Activate company email and join Slack, the primary communication platform.
- Set a strong password that meets the Information Security Policy requirements.
- Review and acknowledge the Employee Handbook and Code of Conduct.

## First Week

During the first week, new employees are expected to complete required trainings and meet their team:

- **Security Awareness Training** — Covers phishing, passwords, and data classification.
- **Code of Conduct Training** — Covers respectful workplace behavior and reporting channels.
- **Anti-Harassment Training** — Required for all employees.

All three required trainings must be completed within the first week of employment.

## Buddy Program

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 90 days. The buddy is an experienced team member who answers questions, provides context, and helps the new hire navigate company tools and culture. The buddy is not the new employee's manager.

## The 30/60/90 Day Plan

New employees work with their manager to create a 30/60/90 day plan that sets clear goals:

- **First 30 days** — Learn the team, tools, and codebase; complete onboarding tasks.
- **First 60 days** — Begin contributing to projects with support.
- **First 90 days** — Work independently and own deliverables.

## Probationary Period

New employees are subject to a 90-day introductory period during which performance and fit are evaluated. Continuous feedback is provided throughout this period in addition to the formal review cycles described in the Employee Handbook.

## Getting Help

For setup issues, new employees should contact the IT Service Desk. For benefits or payroll questions, they should contact the People Operations team. General questions can be directed to the onboarding buddy or the new hire's manager.

# TechCorp Travel and Expense Policy (ARCHIVED — 2021 Edition)

> **ARCHIVED DOCUMENT.** This is the former travel and expense policy and has been **superseded** by the current Travel and Expense Policy. The figures below reflect the **previous** rates and are retained for historical reference only.

## Booking (Former Policy)

Under the former policy, travel was booked by emailing the office administrator. The Concur booking tool was introduced later and was not part of this previous process.

## Per Diem and Lodging (Former Policy)

Previously, the domestic per diem was **$50 per day** and the international per diem was **$75 per day**. The former nightly hotel cap was **$200 per night** for domestic travel. These old rates have since been increased.

## Mileage and Receipts (Former Policy)

Under the old policy, personal vehicle mileage was reimbursed at **$0.58 per mile**. Receipts were previously required only for expenses over **$50**. Reimbursements were formerly processed within **15 business days**. These former terms no longer apply.

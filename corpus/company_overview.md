# TechCorp Company Overview

TechCorp is a fictional software company created for educational and demonstration purposes. This overview summarizes the company's history, leadership, structure, and mission for new and existing employees.

## About TechCorp

TechCorp was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with additional remote-friendly teams distributed across multiple time zones. As of the latest update, TechCorp employs approximately 800 employees across engineering, product, design, sales, marketing, and operations.

TechCorp builds cloud-based developer tooling and data infrastructure products used by businesses worldwide. The company is committed to maintaining a professional, inclusive, secure, and high-performing workplace environment for all employees.

## Mission and Values

TechCorp's mission is to make software development faster, safer, and more collaborative. The company's core values guide decision-making and professional conduct:

- **Customer focus** — Employees prioritize customer outcomes and long-term trust.
- **Ownership** — Employees take responsibility for their work and its impact on business objectives.
- **Transparency** — Important technical decisions and project updates are documented clearly.
- **Inclusion** — TechCorp is an equal opportunity employer and values diverse perspectives.
- **Continuous learning** — Employees are encouraged to grow their skills and share knowledge.

## Leadership Team

TechCorp is led by an experienced executive team:

- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO):** Jordan Avery, who co-founded TechCorp in 2015 and sets overall company strategy.
- **Chief Technology Officer (CTO):** Morgan Lee, who oversees engineering, security, and infrastructure.
- **Chief Operating Officer (COO):** Priya Nair, who leads operations, finance, and people functions.
- **Vice President of Engineering:** Sam Carter, who manages the engineering organization and on-call practices.

Questions for company leadership may be submitted through the monthly all-hands meeting or directly to the People Operations team.

## Company Structure

TechCorp is organized into product-aligned engineering teams, each supported by product management and design. Teams operate Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, with adjusted collaboration hours for employees in other time zones. Slack is the primary communication platform used across all teams.

## Office Locations

TechCorp's primary office is located in Austin, Texas. Employees are permitted to work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with additional remote flexibility approved by department managers. The company maintains satellite collaboration spaces that eligible employees may reserve in advance through the Facilities team.

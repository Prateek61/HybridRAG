# TechCorp IT and Information Security Policy

This policy defines the information security requirements that all TechCorp employees must follow to protect customer and company data. Protecting confidential information is a critical responsibility shared by every employee.

## Account Access and Authentication

TechCorp uses Okta single sign-on (SSO) for access to company systems. Employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all approved systems. Account access is provisioned based on role and the principle of least privilege.

Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Locked accounts may be restored by contacting the IT Service Desk after identity verification.

## Password Requirements

Employees must use strong passwords that meet the following requirements:

- Minimum of 14 characters in length.
- A mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Changed at least every 90 days.
- Never reused across personal and company accounts.

TechCorp provides every employee with a 1Password password manager license. Employees are required to store work credentials in the approved password manager and must never share passwords through email or messaging platforms.

## Device Security

All company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled. On MacBook Pro devices, FileVault encryption is mandatory and enabled during provisioning. Employees must lock their screens when away from their devices and must not leave devices unattended in public spaces.

Operating systems and security patches must be kept up to date. The IT team manages devices through a mobile device management (MDM) platform that enforces security baselines.

## Data Classification

All company information is classified into one of four levels, and employees must handle each level according to its requirements:

- **Public** — Approved for external release.
- **Internal** — For employees only; not for public distribution.
- **Confidential** — Sensitive business or customer data; access restricted to authorized employees.
- **Restricted** — Highly sensitive data such as credentials or regulated personal data; access tightly controlled and logged.

Confidential and Restricted information must never be shared outside authorized channels.

## Software and Application Use

Employees may only install company-approved software. Requests for new software must be submitted through the IT portal for review and approval. Unauthorized software, browser extensions, or pirated applications are strictly prohibited. The IT team maintains a catalog of pre-approved applications that employees may install without additional approval.

## Phishing and Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, phishing attempt, malware, or data breach must be reported immediately to the Information Security team. Suspected phishing emails should be reported using the "Report Phish" button in the email client. Security incidents must be reported within 1 hour of discovery to security@techcorp so the team can respond quickly.

Employees must never attempt to investigate or contain a suspected breach on their own; the Information Security team will coordinate the response.

## Remote Access and VPN

Employees working remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays, or from any untrusted network, must connect through the approved TechCorp VPN before accessing internal systems. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN enabled. Remote employees must follow all security and confidentiality guidelines described in the Employee Handbook.

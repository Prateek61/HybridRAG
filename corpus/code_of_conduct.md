# TechCorp Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct sets the standards of behavior expected of all TechCorp employees. Employees are expected to maintain respectful and professional behavior in all workplace interactions.

## Respectful Workplace

Harassment, bullying, retaliation, intimidation, discrimination, and inappropriate conduct will not be tolerated under any circumstances. TechCorp is an equal opportunity employer, and employment decisions are based on qualifications, performance, and business requirements. Every employee is responsible for contributing positively to team collaboration and workplace culture.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with the interests of TechCorp. Any actual or potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to the employee's manager or the People Operations team within 30 days of becoming aware of it. Examples include outside employment with a competitor, a financial interest in a vendor, or supervising a family member.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may not accept gifts, entertainment, or other items of value worth more than $100 from any vendor, customer, or business partner. Gifts of any value that could influence a business decision must be declined. Modest, infrequent business meals are generally acceptable and should follow the Travel and Expense Policy.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company and customer information and must never share it outside authorized channels. Confidentiality obligations continue even after employment ends. Handling of sensitive data must follow the data classification rules in the IT and Information Security Policy.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who witness or experience a violation of the Code of Conduct are encouraged to report it. TechCorp provides multiple reporting channels:

- Directly to a manager or the People Operations team.
- Through the anonymous ethics hotline, available 24 hours a day.

TechCorp prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a concern in good faith. Whistleblower protections apply to all such reports.

## Disciplinary Process

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action. The standard progressive disciplinary process includes the following steps, though serious violations may result in immediate termination:

1. **Verbal warning** — An informal discussion documenting the concern.
2. **Written warning** — A formal written notice describing the issue and expectations.
3. **Final written warning** — A last opportunity to correct behavior.
4. **Termination** — Ending employment when prior steps have not resolved the issue or when a serious violation occurs.
